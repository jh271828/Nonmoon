## 빈칸 문제

1. What are the three tiers employed by modern IC factories to control unwanted impurities?
   - 답: Clean rooms, Wafer cleaning, and Human control
2. In the SIA Roadmap, what do the acronyms LLS and GOI stand for?
   - 답: LLS stands for Localized Light Scattering, and GOI stands for Gate Oxide Integrity
3. What are the four main types of contaminants that may be found on a wafer?
   - 답: Particles, organic films (photoresist), heavy metals, and alkali ions
4. A shift in MOS threshold voltage (Vth) is typically caused by which type of contamination?
   - 답: Alkali ions (mobile charge QM)
5. Which deep-level impurities are normally associated with traps that degrade the generation lifetime in MOS DRAMs?
   - 답: Au, Cu, Fe, etc.
6. What does 'Class X' mean regarding cleanroom air quality?
   - 답: It means there are less than X total particles greater than 0.5 μm in size per cubic foot of air
7. What is the typical air quality class of a state-of-the-art clean room?
   - 답: Class 1
8. What type of filters are used to clean the air in factory environments?
   - 답: HEPA (High Efficiency Particulate Air) filters
9. What is the standard chemical cleaning process used to remove organics, heavy metals, and alkali ions?
   - 답: RCA clean
10. By what mechanism are particles largely removed from wafer surfaces during cleaning?
   - 답: Ultrasonic agitation
11. What two methods are specified for removing organic films like photoresists?
   - 답: O2 plasma or H2SO4/H2O2 solutions
12. To remove metal atoms from a silicon wafer surface, they must be converted into what state?
   - 답: Ions soluble in the cleaning solution
13. What chemical process is required to convert metal atoms into soluble ions during cleaning?
   - 답: Oxidation (removing electrons from the metal atoms)
