## 빈칸 문제

1. What characterizes a crystal structure?
   - 답: A unit cell which repeats in the x, y, and z directions.
2. How are Miller indices defined for identifying crystal planes?
   - 답: They are the reciprocals of the intercepts of the plane with the x, y, and z axes.
3. Describe the basic diamond crystal structure of Silicon.
   - 답: It consists of two merged FCC cells offset by a/4 in x, y, and z.
4. How does the zincblende structure differ from the diamond structure?
   - 답: It differs only in that there are two different types of atoms in the lattice.
5. Why are most MOS devices fabricated on (100) wafers instead of (111) wafers?
   - 답: Because the (111) surface has higher densities of interface states due to a higher number of dangling bonds.
6. Why have many bipolar transistors been fabricated on (111) wafers?
   - 답: Because the (111) orientation is easier to grow by the Czochralski method.
7. Which silicon plane orientation has the largest number of silicon atoms per unit area?
   - 답: The (111) plane.
8. Classify vacancies (V) and interstitials (I) by their defect dimensionality.
   - 답: They are 0-D defects (point defects).
9. What dimensional defect is a stacking fault?
   - 답: It is a 2-D defect.
10. What are the three main control parameters in the Czochralski crystal growth process?
   - 답: Pull rate, melt temperature, and rotation rate.
