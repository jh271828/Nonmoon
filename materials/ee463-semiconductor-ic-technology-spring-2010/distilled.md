## 빈칸 문제

1. Why are most MOS devices fabricated on (100) wafers instead of (111) wafers?
   - 답: Because the (111) surface has higher densities of interface states due to a higher number of dangling bonds.
2. How does the zincblende structure differ from the diamond structure?
   - 답: It differs only in that there are two different types of atoms in the lattice instead of one.
3. What is the physical configuration of the diamond crystal structure of Silicon?
   - 답: It consists of two merged FCC cells offset by a/4 in the x, y, and z directions.
4. Why have many bipolar transistors been fabricated on (111) wafers?
   - 답: Because the (111) orientation is easier to grow by the Czochralski method.
5. What are the dimensional classifications of crystal defects (0-D, 1-D, 2-D, 3-D)?
   - 답: 0-D defects are vacancies (V) and interstitials (I), 1-D is dislocation, 2-D is stacking fault, and 3-D are agglomerations or precipitates.
6. How are Miller indices defined for crystal planes?
   - 답: They are defined as the reciprocals of the intercepts of the plane with the x, y, and z axes.
7. What are the three main control parameters in Czochralski crystal growth?
   - 답: Pull rate, melt temperature, and rotation rate.
8. What does a (111) wafer orientation mean regarding the crystal surface?
   - 답: It means that the crystal terminates at the wafer surface on {111} planes.
9. Which silicon plane, (111) or (100), has the lowest number of silicon atoms per unit area?
   - 답: The (100) plane.
10. What starting material is melted to begin the Czochralski crystal growth process?
   - 답: Polysilicon material.
